## Stone sequence

`stone/stone-001.webp` to `stone-060.webp` are every second frame of the first stone from the
Mineral Dial project's own render (three.js, procedural ore shaders), at 560 px. The stone's shape
comes from a CC0 Poly Haven scan (Rock 07 by Jenelle van Heerden, https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07).
